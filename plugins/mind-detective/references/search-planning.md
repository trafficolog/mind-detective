# Search-planning reference

A candidate check is a physical action, not a probability claim. It records target, route relation, check state, effort, safety, urgency relevance, basis and provenance.

Before choosing another action, record the user's completed check with the method actually used: `glance`, `visual_systematic`, `empty_and_check`, `tactile`, or `inaccessible`; record `found`, `not_found`, `partial`, or `inaccessible` and any inaccessible parts.

Repeated targets remain visible. Near-duplicate recognition is only a cognitive-load aid. A miss does not become “the item is not there,” and multiple misses are not treated as independent evidence by default.

The planner applies categorical filters and returns one action. Do not translate its output into a percentage, total score, Bayesian posterior, POD or hidden belief weight.
