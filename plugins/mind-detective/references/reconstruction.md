# Reconstruction reference

Start with a free account such as: “Расскажите своими словами всё, что помните с последнего момента, когда предмет точно участвовал в ваших действиях, до момента, когда вы заметили пропажу.” Do not list candidate rooms, containers or actions in that first prompt.

After the free account, clarify one unsupported gap at a time. Prefer neutral forms: ask what happened next, what the user directly remembers, what is routine rather than episodic, and whether a time is exact or approximate. Preserve `None`/unknown when the user does not know.

A location already supplied by the user may be clarified. A new concrete location must not be introduced as a reconstruction cue. If it is useful as a physical search idea, transition to search planning and label it as a proposal.

Never reinterpret confidence, vivid sensory detail, emotion, lighting/color, or repetition as independent verification of truth.
