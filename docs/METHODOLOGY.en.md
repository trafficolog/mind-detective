# Methodology and scientific posture

[Русский](METHODOLOGY.md)

MIND Detective uses memory research primarily to define **safe dialogue constraints**, not to manufacture household location probabilities.

Transferred product principles include free account before detailed clarification, caution with suggestive/misinforming prompts, separation of subjective confidence from verification, preservation of explicit uncertainty and contradictions, avoidance of causal forgetting-mechanism claims, and recording physical search quality instead of converting misses into probabilities.

The Cognitive Interview was developed and tested in witness/victim recall domains. MIND Detective transfers selected neutral-elicitation principles as product methodology and does not claim a clinically or forensically validated household lost-item recovery effect.

## Key sources

- Fisher, R. P., Geiselman, R. E., & Amador, M. (1989). *Field test of the Cognitive Interview*. DOI `10.1037/0021-9010.74.5.722`.
- Loftus, E. F. (2005). *Planting misinformation in the human mind*. DOI `10.1101/lm.94705`.
- Wiechert et al. (2026). *The misinformation effect: A contemporary replication and extension of Loftus et al. (1978) to investigate its underlying mechanisms*. DOI `10.1037/xlm0001529`.
- Ongchoco, J. D. K., & Xu, Y. (2024). *Visual event boundaries trigger forgetting despite active maintenance in visual working memory*. DOI `10.1167/jov.24.9.9`. Event boundaries are not converted into location weights.

All four DOI records were re-verified on 2026-09-12. Machine-readable verification metadata is stored in [`REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.json`](REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.json).

Planner numeric rules are operational policy rather than a scientifically calibrated probability model. The current product contract contains no numeric location model.
